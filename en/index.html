About us

Precision in bleaching, dyeing and finishing.

Johann Müller AG specializes in bleaching, dyeing and finishing yarns, tricot fabrics, terry fabrics and finished garments made from all dyeable materials. Our products are of outstanding quality and enjoy an excellent reputation on the market — our customers include leading international textile and apparel companies.

With a dyeing capacity of over 20,000 kg per day, we are one of the most efficient yarn and tricot finishing companies in Switzerland.

Services

Yarn Dyeing & Piece Dyeing

Two core areas, one standard: the highest quality in textile finishing.

Yarn Dyeing

  • Package dyeing — fully automatic plant, approx. 10,000 kg/day, batches of 5–1,000 kg.
  • Hank dyeing — jet dyeing machines & cabinet apparatus, batches of 10–300 kg, approx. 1,500 kg/day.
  • Winding/Twisting — rewinding as well as two-for-one twisting before or after dyeing.

Piece Dyeing

  • Jet dyeing — fully automatic, approx. 10,000 kg/day, batches of 5–700 kg, including terry fabrics.
  • Garment dyeing — finished garments in batches up to 75 kg.
  • Finishing — complete machinery for tubular and open-width fabric (up to 2.40 m), including raising, shearing and grinding.

Materials: Cotton, viscose, linen, wool, silk, polyester, polyamide, polyacrylonitrile and blends thereof — staple-fibre and filament yarns.

History

Five Generations, One Family Business

From a yarn and cotton-cloth printing works in 1845 to one of the most efficient yarn and tricot finishing companies in Switzerland — a journey through five generations of the Müller family.

  • 1845Founded as a yarn and cotton-cloth printing works in Strengelbach.
  • 1892Relocation to the current site due to abundant water sources.
  • 1917/1939Expansion with a knitting mill and a branch in Wohlen.
  • 1945100th anniversary, already 540 employees.
  • 1993World's first textile finishing company certified to OEKO-TEX® Standard 100.
  • 2001Switch to renewable energy sources, awarded environmental prizes.
  • 2002The fifth generation joins the management.
  • TodayInnovative in-house products such as SwissCotton Rainbow and SwissCotton BeDry.
